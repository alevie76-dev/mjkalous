Bound in chains of self-regret, I find myself alone. Accusing sins strangling hope that I can e're atone. Heavy tears fall to the ground crushing heart and bone. I have no voice, my breath is gone. Only God, of His own will, can reconstruct my heart. And only He can free my soul, and cleanse and take me home.
