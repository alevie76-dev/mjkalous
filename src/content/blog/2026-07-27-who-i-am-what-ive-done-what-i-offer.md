It has taken me weeks to figure out how to recover and become productive again after a vicious virus attack on my computer. This is a new computer and a new email address. I write this note today with gratitude for the freedom I feel in once again being able to post a part of my mind onto the page. Lately, there has been much discussion regarding the use of AI in the art of writing. I want it known that I do not use AI to write or create my content. I believe this is a personal choice, like whether to indulge in this or that. I have had the benefit of having a phisher-person create a banner for my book, which looks okay and is informative; however, I would not have chosen this path for myself. I am not going to look a gift-horse in the mouth. If you don't know what this refers to, ask me and I will explain it for you. If you are interested in what the banner looks like, and have stayed with me this far, see the image above. May the Lord bless you in your work and life. Michael
