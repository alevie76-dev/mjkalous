A few months ago, I stepped into a world of electronics, videos, and digital platforms—and I was terrified. This month, I'm grateful. And I want to share what I'm learning with you.

Thank you to all of you who are following me on this journey. I have stepped into a whole new world and feel lost much of the time. Those who know me, know that my relationship with electronics often results in the death of a watch or computer. I have heard so many times a store clerk say to me: "This has never happened before," as her computer screen goes into a spiral of activity going nowhere. I have walked under streetlamps that have turned on or off as I walk by. I walk into my bedroom through the living room and the TV turns on, though I am four feet away from it. So, taking this step into the realm where electronics, computers, phones, and media exist is a risk for those who work near me. I am not allowed anywhere near the computer at my church. That's okay. I don't want to be there anyway.

However, here I am making videos, keeping a blog, a website, a Facebook Page, and I'm not sure what else! I extend my heartfelt appreciation to Allen of OpsRelay, who helps me survive as I walk through this valley of the shadow of the Prince of the Power of the Air. Overall, I have truly been enjoying this new venture and I have only killed one computer so far.

On this trip, I have had two book signings, interviewed on three podcasts, and will be doing a book signing in Great Falls in a few weeks (I will include the information below) with more to follow. I am now beginning to introduce poetry into some of my writings. I have always enjoyed writing poetry and I hope it entertains you, perhaps touches your heart, and piques your interest.

I included some **poetry** in my book and I thought perhaps you might like to read one of those in this newsletter.

> **Elemental Motion**
> Breezes playing, dancing
> Leaves shimmering, shaking and shifting
> shades of green, silver, and gray.
> Waves alternating greens, whites, and blues,
> swirling wisps of foam and mist.
> Sands moving, snakelike along the desert
> dry-baked alkali flats and barren lakes.
> These moving elements
> drift through my mind
> reminding me of the life I lived
> in constant motion
> subject to the wind of will and moment
> within boundary of only space or time
> and hindered only by the limits
> of my willingness / to move.

### Exiting the Past to Enter the Future

[![An open, weathered red door in an old log cabin](/uploads/newsletter/november-2026/exiting-the-past.jpg)](/blog/exiting-the-past-to-enter-the-future)

*The HERO Construct*

My dad drove home the message that I would be "no good" and "worthless." That curse came true for my brothers. It didn't come true for me.

Why? I had something they didn't: **the HERO Construct.** These four things helped me overcome the curses of childhood and find hope:

- **H**ero: Someone from whom to borrow hope, courage, and strength
- **E**xpression: A way to release negative messages (through art, music, dance, sports, etc.)
- **R**efuge: A safe place—physical, mental, or spiritual—to escape the harm
- **O**utlet: Giving to others the love you didn't receive

"Good gathers good. Evil compounds evil. We must choose which way we go."

### Is It Love or Is It Respect?

[![Open Montana prairie under heavy clouds](/uploads/newsletter/november-2026/love-vs-respect.jpg)](/blog/love-vs-respect)

*The distinction that changes everything*

"Your respect gives me purpose." A husband said this to his wife mid-conversation, and it changed how she understood their relationship.

Here's the question it raised: **Can I genuinely love someone I don't presently respect?** The answer lies in understanding the difference: **"Love is given. Respect is earned."**

- **Love:** Everyone deserves it. It's about being known, heard, accepted.
- **Respect:** Developed over time, based on character, choices, conduct.

The hard truth: You can love someone while not respecting their choices. And boundaries are part of love.

**The real question:** Can love create an opportunity for respect to grow again?
